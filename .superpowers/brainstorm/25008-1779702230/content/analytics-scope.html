Link Analytics — What Stats?

What data do you want to see per link? (multi-select)

1

Clicks Over Time

Line chart showing clicks per day/week/month. Ability to filter by date range.

2

Referrers / Sources

Where clicks are coming from (Twitter, LinkedIn, direct, etc.)

3

Devices & Browsers

Desktop vs mobile vs tablet. Chrome, Safari, Firefox breakdown.

4

Geographic Locations

Country/city breakdown of where clicks originate.

5

Total Clicks (Simple Counter)

Just a total click count per link, no extra tracking. Simplest to implement.

6

Unique Click Tracking

Deduplicate clicks by IP or fingerprint to show unique visitor counts.
